Matching order. Exact normalised planning reference; planning-reference token; ACP case reference; exact authority; Eircode; address similarity; point-to-point distance; grant-date and unit compatibility. A completion certificate inherits the planning match of its linked commencement notice. Scores of 85–100 are automatic; 65–84 require review; lower scores remain unmatched.
The live NBCO resource is the completion authority. The transformed NBCO dataset is used only as a spatial candidate index when references are missing or malformed. No uncertain match is silently promoted.
