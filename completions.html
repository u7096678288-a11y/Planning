Matching safeguards. The engine normalises the planning reference and ACP reference, requires the local authority to agree for an automatic result, and then rationalises the candidate using the address, Eircode and point-to-point distance. A planning-reference match without location support is held for review. Completion certificates inherit the planning match of their linked commencement notice.
The live NBCO BuildingsCNsCCCs resource is the source of commencement and completion evidence. Duplicate flattened relationship rows are removed before units are totalled. The project’s “First appeared” date is the earliest published submission, validation, commencement or completion date across all linked records.
