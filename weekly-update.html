Coverage rules. The report includes applications reporting at least 100 residential units; ACP cases identified as SID, strategic housing/LRD/SHD or major infrastructure; projects with at least 10,000 m² floor area or 10 hectares; and national-scale transport, energy, utility, port, airport, hospital or data-centre projects identified from the published description/category. A movement is included where a planning receipt, planning decision, grant, appeal lodgement, appeal decision, ACP lodgement or ACP decision falls within the rolling 30-day window.
The report is generated from public ArcGIS and Cork City CKAN feeds. Applicant names, unit figures and appeal links are shown only where published. ACP notes that its mapped dataset may omit some invalid or withdrawn cases; the official case page remains authoritative.
