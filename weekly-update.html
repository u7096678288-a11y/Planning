Coverage. The report uses a rolling 28-day buffer for submissions, FI movements, grants, ACP lodgements and ACP decisions. Ordinary residential projects are included only where the official feed reports at least 100 units. Identifiable SIDs remain included irrespective of residential-unit count. The final Upcoming section contains qualifying applications with a published decision due date in the next 28 days and no recorded decision.
Local-authority road developments, CPOs and rail orders appear only where they meet the 100-unit rule or are identifiable SIDs. Applicant names, unit figures and dates are shown only where present in the official public feeds. The linked planning or ACP record remains authoritative.
